# Sprint 3 (autopilot and scale) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Ship what a phone-only app cannot follow (reminders that send themselves, a customer link, a team with a
collector's route and approvals, several currencies) and open the Business plan, with web checkout and the iPhone app
ready the moment the owner's accounts exist.

**Architecture:** As in Sprints 1 and 2. Each item is a Laravel action with `/api/v1` endpoints (OpenAPI drift-tested),
a web page under `/app`, and Flutter screens. Every item sits behind an admin feature switch and ships *off*; the owner
turns it on in `/admin/features`. Credentials for outside services (SMTP, WhatsApp Business, an SMS provider, a payment
gateway) are entered by the owner in `/admin`, stored encrypted, never shown back, and audited without their values;
until they exist, each channel uses a driver that only logs, as push does today. Money keeps its rules: bcmath strings,
`decimal(18,4)`, ledger rows never edited, corrections by reversal. Every migration runs on PostgreSQL (PGlite) before
it is pushed.

**Tech Stack:** Laravel 13, Pest, Pint, Larastan 6; Flutter (Riverpod, go_router, dio); Supabase Postgres; the Meta
WhatsApp Cloud API; StoreKit 2 and the App Store Server API; GitHub Actions.

**Spec:** `docs/features/win-plan.html` (6.3 K2, K6, K7, K10, K11, K12; 6.8 Sprint 3; gap rows NT02, NT03, NT04, CM03,
CM04, CM09, CT25, IN09, SC05, SC07, SC10, BK08, BK09, PL02, PL09, PP14). Sprint 2 shipped on 12 Oct 2026 (Tasks 1–9).
**This plan waits for the owner's review and "go ahead" before Task 2 starts** (Task 1 builds a choice the owner already
made on 10 Oct 2026: e-mail through their own SMTP).

## Decisions (defaults the owner can change)

| # | Question | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | E-mail | The owner's own SMTP server, entered in `/admin/mail` (host, port, encryption, user, password, sender name and address), stored encrypted in the platform settings and applied to the mailer at boot. A "send a test" button. Environment variables still win when set, so a server-level setup keeps working. |
| D2 | The Business plan | A third plan, `business`, created **hidden** (not on the pricing page) with no price until the owner sets one in `/admin/plans` (Win Plan 6.4). It includes everything in Pro, plus automatic reminders, unlimited members, collector mode and approvals. Pro keeps the one-tap remind-all (PP9). |
| D3 | Reminder cadence | Per workspace: 3 days before, on the day, 2 days after and 7 days after, each editable (days) or switched off. One message per instalment per step, ever (a unique row), and none once the instalment is paid or the contract settled, cancelled or written off. |
| D4 | Reminder stages and wording | Before and on the day: friendly. After 2 days: firm. After 7 days: to the guarantor as well, when the contract has one and Guarantors is on. A final notice is never automatic: the owner sends it. Each stage has editable wording in the customer's language (the existing editable wording, PP9), with the amount, date, contract and shop name filled in. |
| D5 | Consent and quiet hours | No automatic message reaches a customer without a recorded consent (who recorded it, when, and how: in person, in the signed contract, or through the customer link). Customers can stop messages (WhatsApp "stop", or the customer link). Nothing is sent between 21:00 and 09:00 in the workspace's time zone (editable); a step due at night waits for the morning. |
| D6 | Channels | WhatsApp through the Meta **WhatsApp Cloud API** directly (no reseller), with message templates the owner gets approved in Meta's Business Manager; template names and the phone-number id and token are entered in `/admin/whatsapp`. SMS through a driver interface with no provider at launch (the owner's choice of 10 Oct: decide later); when WhatsApp fails or the customer has no WhatsApp, SMS is tried if a provider exists. The owner gets a push when a step could not be sent. |
| D7 | Promises to pay | Reminder templates carry three reply buttons: "I'll pay today", "In 3 days", "Call me". A reply arrives through Meta's webhook (signature checked with the app secret) and records a promise (date, channel) on the instalment; reminders pause until the promised date; a broken promise is shown on the contract and in the Today screen. Promises can also be recorded by hand. |
| D8 | The "collected within 48 hours" line | A monthly figure: money received within 48 hours after an automatic reminder for that instalment, labelled exactly so, never "caused by reminders". |
| D9 | The customer link | One active link per customer, a random token stored hashed, valid 90 days, revocable, `noindex`, in the customer's language (or the shop's). It shows the shop's name and logo, what is owed, the schedule (with moved dates), receipts as PDFs, and promises. Never a national ID, a guarantor or another customer. "Ask for more time" sends the owner a request with a proposed date; "I've paid" uploads a photo of the transfer as a **proof the owner reviews**: it never records money. Pro and Business. |
| D10 | Collector mode | Customers can be assigned to a collector. The collector's route is today's and overdue instalments of their customers, nearest first by the order the collector sets; a daily target is shown. A cash handover is the collector's declaration of the cash they hold; the owner or a manager confirms it, and any difference from the payments recorded is flagged and audited. |
| D11 | Approvals | When a collector or an accountant voids a payment, waives a late fee or writes off a contract, it becomes a request the owner or a manager approves or declines; nothing in the ledger changes until it is approved. Owners and managers act directly, as today. The activity log (already built) shows requests and decisions. Business plan. |
| D12 | Several currencies | A workspace lists the currencies it uses (its main one first). A contract has one currency, chosen when it is made; payments are in the contract's currency. Totals are **never added across currencies**: dashboards, reports and exports show one figure per currency. An investor has a currency; money moved between an investor and a contract in another currency carries the rate typed on that day, kept with the line. No outside exchange-rate feed. |
| D13 | Zakat estimate | Per investor: the cash they hold with the business plus what is owed to them on active contracts that is expected to be collected (overdue by 90 days or less), at 2.5% for a lunar year, with the method, the date and the lunar-year rate shown, and the words "an estimate to review with a scholar". Behind its own switch, **off until a scholar has reviewed the method** (owner action). |
| D14 | Phone sign-in | Sign-in by phone number with a six-digit code sent by SMS or a WhatsApp authentication template, whichever exists; until one does, the option is not shown. Codes are hashed, expire in 10 minutes and allow 5 tries. Changing the e-mail address keeps everything (a confirmation to the new address, a notice to the old). The device list with remote sign-out (built on the web) comes to the app. |
| D15 | Google Drive copy | A weekly copy of the workspace's books (the existing nightly Excel copy) to the owner's Google Drive, through Google's OAuth with the narrowest scope (`drive.file`). Needs the owner's Google Cloud OAuth client; until then the option is not shown. |
| D16 | Tablet layouts | The app uses two panes on screens 840 dp and wider: lists beside details for customers, contracts and payments. |
| D17 | Web checkout | A gateway driver with **Moyasar** first (mada, cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay for Saudi Arabia), Tap and HyperPay to follow behind the same driver. The website's billing page pays for Pro or Business monthly or yearly; the gateway's webhook (signature checked) activates the plan, idempotently. Never offered or linked inside the Android or iPhone app. Regional prices: each plan can have a price per country, set in `/admin/plans`, shown by the visitor's country. Bank transfer stays as built (PP13). |
| D18 | The iPhone and Pro | StoreKit 2 in the iPhone app, with the same plan keys as Google Play; the server checks each purchase with the App Store Server API and takes App Store Server Notifications v2 (renewals, refunds, expiry). Until the Apple account exists the iPhone app shows plans with no way to buy and no link out (App Review 3.1.1). |
| D19 | Offline receipt drafts (PP14) | **Not in this sprint** unless the owner decides for them (Win Plan 6.9): a receipt written offline would need a rule for what happens when two people record the same payment. |

## Tasks

### Task 1: E-mail through the owner's SMTP (D1)

**Files:** `App\Mail\MailSettings` (platform settings `mail.*`, password encrypted with Crypt), a service provider hook
that applies them to `config('mail.mailers.smtp')` and `mail.default`/`mail.from` when no `MAIL_MAILER` environment
variable is set; `App\Http\Controllers\Admin\MailController` (show, update, test), view `admin/mail/index.blade.php`,
routes `admin.mail.*`, admin nav entry; the admin overview's e-mail check reads the same settings.

- [x] Tests: saved settings send verification and password-reset mails through SMTP (Mail fake on the transport); the
  password is encrypted at rest and never shown back; only a super admin can change them; the test button sends one
  mail to the admin and reports a failure in plain words; environment variables, when set, win; the overview says
  "Sending through smtp"; saving and removing are audited without the password.
- [x] Implement, translations; commit, push.

- [x] Tests: a notice goes in the app by default, by e-mail once SMTP is saved and the address verified, never by text
