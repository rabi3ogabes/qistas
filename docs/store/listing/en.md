## Title

Qistas: Installment Tracker

## Short description

Installment sales and debts in one ledger, with reminders. On phone and web.

## Full description

Qistas keeps a shop's installment sales in order: every customer, every contract and every payment, to the cent.

• Every plan worked out for you: the down payment, the profit and equal installments that add up to the total exactly.
• Payments land on the oldest installment first. A mistake is undone with a reversal, never erased.
• Today's screen: what is due today, who is late and what comes next.
• Remind a customer on WhatsApp in one tap, with the amount and the date already written.
• Statements and receipts as PDF with your shop's logo, to share or print.
• Reports: what is coming in, who is late, and profit as money comes in.
• Your books in the cloud, saved the moment you record a payment and copied every night.
• Fingerprint lock and two-step sign-in.
• The same account on your phone and on the web.
• Arabic, English, French, Spanish and Urdu, right to left where it should be, with a dark mode.

Free for your first 20 customers, with no ads. Pro when you need more.

## When switched on

- `instalment_alerts`: • Remind everyone due today in one tap, on WhatsApp, in your customer's language.
- `flexible_schedules`: • Any schedule: daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, yearly or your own dates, up to 600 installments.
- `investors`: • Investors: each one's capital, money out in contracts, and profit as customers pay.
- `members`: • Your partner, accountant and collectors on the same books, each with the right permissions.
- `import`: • Bring your customers in from Excel or CSV, with a trial run before anything is saved.
- `ai_reading`: • Photograph a notebook page and your customers are read in, ready for you to check.
- `guarantors`: • Guarantors, contract photos and the customer's signature, kept with the contract.
- `hijri_dates`: • Hijri dates beside the Gregorian ones.
- `late_fees`: • Late fees by your own rule, kept apart from the installments.
