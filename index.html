Test Qistas, live.

Three working prototypes of the Qistas platform for merchants and lenders: the animated intro in five languages, the admin Theme Studio that recolours the product for every country and event, and the brand system behind them. Everything runs in your browser. No sign-in, nothing is sent anywhere.

  • العربية · English · Français · Español · اردو
  • Right-to-left ready
  • 26 themeable colours
  • WCAG AA gate
