01 · The name

A word that already means fair.

Qistas is the classical Arabic word for the true scale. It carries qist, the everyday word for an instalment in Arabic and Urdu, and the older sense of the same root: equity, justice, a fair share.

قسطاس

The balance

The scale that does not cheat. Known across Arabic and Urdu, easy to say in French and Spanish once you hear it: KIS-tas, with the q a deep k.

قسط

The instalment

One fair share of a larger sum, paid on time. The product's daily work, in the word.

=

The promise

Lending that is fair to both sides, with clear terms, honest records and an interest-free mode when you need it.

Naming checks

  • Distinct from "Qist". The short form is already used by Qist Bazaar, a funded Pakistani buy-now-pay-later platform. The longer classical word avoids a direct clash.
  • Web search, 7 Oct 2026: no existing "Qistas" fintech or instalment brand turned up.
  • Not a trademark clearance. Before launch run a professional search (WIPO, EUIPO, USPTO, GCC and Pakistan registries) and a reverse-image search of the mark. The name stays a white-label setting, as the master prompt requires.
02 · The logo

Plumb q.

A plumb-bob finds true. The descender of the q is the line and the gold bob is the weight: the level, honest measure that a balance stands for. In Arabic, the two dots of the qaf become two gold coins.

Construction

The rules of the drawing

  • Letters are built from circles, arcs and rectangles on one grid: x-height 96, stroke 14 (0.146 of x-height), round overshoot 1.2, no live text, no strokes in the masters.
  • The bob has radius 15.5, a little more than the stem is wide, so it reads as a weight at 16 px.
  • Gold appears once in the Latin logo (the bob) and twice in the Arabic (the dots). Gold is never used for the letters themselves.
  • Arabic is Cairo Regular, outlined, weight-matched to the Latin (stroke ÷ body height 0.142 against 0.146).
  • Small sizes use the heavier symbol cut (stroke 34 on a 140 ring, bob radius 28). A 16 px test, a squint test and a one-colour test all pass.
  • Readings checked: mirrored it becomes a p, rotated 180° a b. Neither is offensive or confusing.

Clear space & minimum size

Minimum sizes

Clear space = one bob diameter on every side. Wordmark never below 22 px tall; symbol never below 16 px.

05 · Voice

Calm, exact, dignified.

Money is personal. Qistas speaks like a trusted private banker: brief, precise, never alarming, and never shaming. A late customer is a customer, not a "defaulter".

Calm

No exclamation marks in money moments. Confirmation is quiet and quick.

Exact

Amounts, dates and counts, never "soon" or "a lot". Say what happened and what is next.

Dignified

Reminders are firm, never humiliating. The same respect in every language and tone.

Plain

One idea per sentence. No jargon. "Instalment", not "amortisation tranche".

One reminder, three tones (the lender chooses)

Friendly

Hi Mona, a quick reminder: your instalment of SAR 450 is due tomorrow. Thank you!

مرحبًا منى، تذكير لطيف: قسطكِ البالغ 450 ر.س يستحق غدًا. شكرًا لكِ!

Formal

Dear Ms. Salem, this is a reminder that your instalment of SAR 450 falls due on 8 October. Kind regards, Al-Fares Electronics.

Firm

Dear Ms. Salem, your instalment of SAR 450 was due on 8 October and is now 7 days overdue. Please settle it by 15 October, or contact us to agree a plan.

Say

Payment recorded. Balance SAR 1,350.

We could not save that. Your data is safe. Try again.

Avoid

06 · Motion

The line pays out. The weight settles.

The intro plays once, at cold start: the ring is drawn, the plumb-bob falls on a spring and pulls the line down, a ripple marks the landing, then the wordmark forms around the q. Everywhere else, motion is quick and quiet.

Latin intro · 3.3 s

Arabic & Urdu intro · 2.9 s

Ingredients

FrequencyRare (cold start). The only place the delight budget is spent.
PurposeDelight and explanation: true, level, fair.
Propertiestransform, opacity; stroke-dashoffset (line draw); mask-position (Arabic wipe).
Easingease-out cubic-bezier(.23,1,.32,1) · ease-in-out cubic-bezier(.77,0,.175,1)
Springmass 1 · stiffness 90 · damping 11 (≈ 10% overshoot, settles in ~0.9 s)
UI motion140 / 220 / 360 ms. Under 300 ms for anything the user triggers.
HapticOne soft tick when the bob lands (native only).
Reduced motionFinal frame fades in over 240 ms and holds for 1.1 s. No movement.
Never animateKeyboard shortcuts, command palette, tab switches, anything done 100+ times a day.
